## 关于

这是一本个人文集，收录了十一篇写于不同时间的文章与随笔，按四个篇章重新编排：从生成式 AI 对认知的放大，到法兰克福学派与身份政治的检讨，再到行走途中的见证，最后落回一份写给自己的成长法则。

跨度看似很大，但贯穿其中的是同一个问题：当理性不足以解释世界时，一个人该如何自处。

**About** — A personal collection of eleven essays and reflections, arranged into four parts: the amplification of cognition by generative AI, a re-examination of the Frankfurt School and identity politics, observations gathered while travelling, and finally a set of personal principles written at twenty. Two essays are in English; the rest are in Chinese.
